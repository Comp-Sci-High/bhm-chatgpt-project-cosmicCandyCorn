Influential Black Directors

Ava DuVernay

Known for Selma, When They See Us, and 13th.

Spike Lee

Iconic filmmaker behind Do the Right Thing and BlacKkKlansman.

Barry Jenkins

Director of Moonlight and If Beale Street Could Talk.

Dee Rees

Director of Pariah, Mudbound, and Bessie.

Ryan Coogler

Known for Black Panther, Fruitvale Station, and Creed.

Kasi Lemmons

Director of Eve’s Bayou and Harriet.

Janet Mock

First trans woman of color to direct an episode of television (Pose).

Radha Blank

Writer-director of The Forty-Year-Old Version.

Justin Simien

Created Dear White People and directed Bad Hair.

Jenn Nkiru

Experimental filmmaker known for Black to Techno and Beyoncé’s Black Is King.
